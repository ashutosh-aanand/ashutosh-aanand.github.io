## SK Chess

*wxPython · python-chess · Stockfish · React · Tailwind CSS*
&nbsp;&nbsp;[accessiblechess.in](https://accessiblechess.in)

An accessible chess application built for visually impaired players. It reads
PGN books aloud, gives real-time audio feedback on the position, runs Stockfish
analysis, and supports a training mode, manual move input and game recording —
all navigable without sight.

Players in **10+ countries** use it, and **ChessBase India** featured it for
advancing accessibility and inclusion in chess.

## Impression Capping Platform

*Java · Spring Boot · Redis · Kafka*

A service that regulates how often a given banner is shown to a given user
across Paytm's business verticals, at **100K+ impressions per second**. The hard
part is not the counting — it's counting fast enough, and being approximately
right at the edges rather than exactly wrong at the centre.
