## SK Chess

*wxPython, python-chess, Stockfish, React, Tailwind* — [accessiblechess.in](https://accessiblechess.in)

A chess application for visually impaired players. It reads PGN books aloud,
gives audio feedback on the position, runs Stockfish analysis, and supports a
training mode, manual move input and game recording — all usable without
sight.

Players in more than ten countries use it. ChessBase India wrote about it.
